## Overview

Khet is a UX/UI portfolio project focused on a simple question:

> **How might we make agricultural information easier to understand and act on for people using smartphones in low-resource environments?**

The concept explores a human-centered agricultural companion designed around:

- Simple information architecture
- Low digital-literacy UX
- Hindi-first interaction
- Accessibility
- Mobile-first design
- Voice and photo-based input
- Responsible AI interaction
- Offline-aware experiences
- Clear, actionable guidance

Rather than creating another generic AI chatbot, Khet focuses on **task-oriented assistance**: helping a user identify what they need, provide information in a way that feels natural, understand AI-assisted information, and decide what to do next.

> **Important:** Khet is a conceptual UX/UI prototype and is not an official agricultural service, government product, or diagnostic tool.

# 🎯 The Design Problem

Agricultural applications can become difficult to use when they rely on:

- Dense information
- Technical terminology
- Long forms
- Small text
- Complex navigation
- Text-heavy interaction
- Constant internet connectivity
- Interfaces designed primarily for highly digitally experienced users

For this concept, the challenge was to reduce unnecessary cognitive load while maintaining user control and dignity.

### Design question

> **How might we help farmers access understandable agricultural information without requiring them to navigate a complicated digital system?**

---

# 👥 Target Users

The primary user is conceptually:

**A farmer using a smartphone in a rural or low-resource setting.**

The design considers users who may:

- Prefer Hindi
- Use an entry-level smartphone
- Experience intermittent connectivity
- Have limited time
- Prefer visual information
- Be more comfortable with familiar language
- Prefer speaking instead of typing
- Have limited experience with complex digital forms
- Use the product outdoors
- Need larger touch targets
- Have difficulty reading very small text

### Design principle

Low digital literacy does **not** mean low intelligence.

The interface therefore reduces unnecessary complexity without making the experience childish or patronizing.

# 🧠 UX Approach

The product was designed around a simple principle:

> **Show less. Explain clearly. Let the user decide what to do next.**

The core journey is:

```text
Welcome
   ↓
Home
   ↓
Choose Crop
   ↓
Describe Problem
   ↓
Photo Input
   ↓
AI-Assisted Information
   ↓
